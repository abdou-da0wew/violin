# Code of Conduct

Violin is maintained as an open-source project. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Give actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect differing experience levels, identities, and backgrounds.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention, or sustained disruption.
- Trolling, insults, or behavior that makes participation hostile.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and communicated clearly where practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub: https://github.com/abdou-da0wew. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to project spaces and interactions where someone is representing or participating in the Violin community.
